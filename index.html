Meet your provider

Care rooted in trust, honesty & clear communication

Ifeoma A. Ubadike is a board‑certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP‑C) and Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP‑BC). She earned her Master of Science in Nursing and an advanced graduate certification in psychiatric & mental health, bringing a background spanning critical‑care nursing and high‑acuity psychiatric settings.

Her approach is collaborative and whole‑person: she takes time to understand your story, partners with you on a plan that fits your life, and provides thoughtful, evidence‑based medication management. “I believe the best client relationships are built on trust, honesty, and clear communication.”
